# Lake house — buy vs. keep renting

A single-file model of whether buying a Leelanau County lake house beats renting one
week a summer for the next 30 years. Open `index.html` in a browser; every assumption
is a slider and the charts, tiles, and year-by-year table recompute live.

## The model

Two scenarios, forced apples-to-apples: each year, whichever path spends less invests
the difference at the market return.

- **Keep renting** — the down payment + closing costs go into the market on day one,
  the summer week is paid at lodging inflation, and the annual gap between owning and
  renting is invested.
- **Buy** — 30-year mortgage, non-homestead property tax (grows at inflation per the
  Michigan taxable-value cap), insurance, maintenance as % of value, utilities, minus
  net short-term-rental income (peak + shoulder weeks, less management and platform
  costs, growing at lodging inflation). Net worth = home value − loan balance +
  invested surplus, optionally less selling costs at the horizon.

Lifestyle value (spontaneous extra weekends the house makes free) is a separate
opt-in lever so the pure-financial answer stays visible.

## Defaults

Grounded in Aug 2026 research (sources in the page footer): $1.2M inland-lake
frontage, 7.1% second-home rate, 1.6% effective non-homestead tax, ~$71.5k gross STR
revenue on 9 peak + 8 shoulder weeks at 25% management. Township STR rules
(Glen Arbor/Leland permissive, Suttons Bay/Elmwood capped, permits often die at sale)
and mortgage-classification caveats are listed in the footer, not modeled.

## When a real purchase gets serious: paid data options (priced Aug 2026)

The occupancy/ADR defaults come from free market-wide summaries (AirDNA, AirROI,
Rabbu free pages), which blend cottages and condos with lakefront 4BRs. For
property-level numbers:

- **Rabbu** — free address-level reports (projected revenue, monthly breakdown, ADR,
  occupancy, comps), no login. First stop for any candidate listing.
- **PriceLabs Market Dashboard** — $9.99/mo for a one-market dashboard (≤1k listings).
- **AirDNA** — $125 for a single month-to-month ($34/mo billed annually): 36 months of
  market history + unlimited address-level Rentalizer reports with custom comp sets.
- **BNBCalc** — 2 free analyses, then $19/mo for unlimited reports with 40 comps.
- Skip: Mashvisor ($50/mo STR tier) and KeyData ($295+/report) add nothing here.

Realistic total to fully underwrite one purchase: $0–135.
