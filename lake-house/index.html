Lake house — buy vs. keep renting

Thirty-year comparison for a Leelanau County place: keep renting a week each summer and invest the difference, or buy, use it freely, and rent it out short-term. Both paths invest whatever cash the other path spends, so the comparison is apples to apples.

Net worth over time

Buy: home equity plus invested surplus. Rent: the down payment and every dollar owning would have cost, invested instead.

Annual out-of-pocket

Owning is net of short-term-rental income; renting is the summer week at lodging inflation.

Where the defaults come from (researched Aug 2026):

Price $1.2M — typical 3–4BR with private inland-lake frontage (Glen Lake, Lake Leelanau); county waterfront average list ~$1.32M, Lake Leelanau median ~$1.04M (Redfin/Zillow). Lake-access without frontage runs ~$350–650k; Lake Michigan frontage ~$1.5M+. Rate 7.1% — Aug 2026 30-yr primary ~6.7% (Freddie Mac) plus the second-home premium; an investment-property classification would run ~7.35% with 25% down. Tax 1.6% — Leelanau non-homestead millage (~32–38 mills, no Principal Residence Exemption) on taxable value that uncaps to ~50% of price at purchase; grows at inflation under the Michigan cap (Michigan Treasury 2024 rates). Insurance $3k, maintenance 1.5% — northern-Michigan waterfront quotes; includes dock in/out and winterization; flood insurance usually not required on inland lakes. Appreciation 4.5% — Traverse City area long-run; 10-yr trailing was ~8.9%/yr but 2025–26 cooled to 1.5–3.6%. STR — area occupancy ~38–53% with 40–45% of revenue in June–Aug; lakefront 4BRs list ~$5–7k/wk peak (a 3BR more like $3–4.5k); ~12 peak weekly slots exist, so 9 rented assumes keeping 2–3 for ourselves; shoulder weeks price near half of peak; full-service management runs 20–30% (AirDNA/Rabbu/AirROI free pages, local managers).

Caveats the sliders don't capture: STR rules are township-by-township — Glen Arbor and Leland are permissive (registration only), Suttons Bay and Elmwood cap permits, and permits often die at sale, so verify the township before buying. Renting more than ~2 weeks/yr while using rental income to qualify can reclassify the mortgage as investment property (higher rate, 25% down). IRS treats it as a personal residence if personal use exceeds the greater of 14 days or 10% of rented days; under 15 rented days/yr the income is tax-free. Michigan's 6% use tax is collected by Airbnb/VRBO automatically. The model ignores income tax on STR profit (depreciation usually shelters much of it) and any tax on investment gains in both scenarios.
